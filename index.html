About

I am a Prime Minister’s Research Fellow (PMRF) at the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, working in experimental high-energy physics. My research focuses on the study of strongly interacting QCD matter created in ultra-relativistic heavy-ion collisions, with particular emphasis on heavy-flavor probes and applications of machine learning in high-energy physics.

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Physics from the University of Delhi and my Master’s degree from IIT Indore before joining the institute for doctoral research. I am presently associated with the ALICE Collaboration at CERN, where I contribute to analyses related to the spin polarization of the φ meson and the development of machine-learning-based particle identification algorithms using the Transition Radiation Detector (TRD). As part of the ALICE Collaboration, I was awarded the 2025 Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics.

Research Interests

Heavy-flavor probes

Transport and diffusion of charm quarks and open-charm hadrons, and fluctuations in the charmed sector as probes of the medium.

Machine learning in HEP

Application of machine learning to classify final state particles carrying heavy-flavor imprints. Development of neural networks to explore the QCD medium.

QCD Thermodynamics

Thermodynamic and transport properties of strongly interacting matter, including the effects of magnetic fields and medium interactions.
